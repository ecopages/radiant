Add `MediaController` and `createMediaController` to `@ecopages/radiant-ui/controllers`, with the `MediaState`, `MediaStatus`, `MediaControllerConfig` and `MediaLoadOptions` types: the playback core of an `HTMLAudioElement` or `HTMLVideoElement`, so a player with custom controls never listens to a media event. It reports one state snapshot derived from the element (`idle`, `loading`, `ready`, `playing`, `paused`, `ended` or `error`, with position, duration, buffered ranges, seekable window, `currentSrc`, volume, rate, `waiting`, `seeking`, `live` and `blocked`), calls `onProgress` once per animation frame while playing, and offers `load` (with `autoplay` and `startAt`), `retry`, `play`, `pause`, `toggle`, `seek`, a debounced `scrub`, `skip`, `goLive` and the volume, mute, rate and loop setters. `play()` never returns a promise that does not settle on an element with no source, treats an interrupted play as quiet and an autoplay refusal as `blocked`, and does nothing after a rejected `resolveSource` until `load()` or `retry()` (`seekable` is `false` meanwhile). An optional `resolveSource` hook loads through a Blob for servers without range requests, with `releaseSource` to revoke it, and a newer `load()` or a `destroy()` discards a stale resolution. `destroy()`, and `attach()` to another element, pause and release in a microtask and only when no controller is attached to the element any more, so a DOM move keeps playing; the resolved source is released once, by the last controller, and only with `clearSourceOnDestroy`: otherwise the element keeps `src` and the next `load()` releases it.

Add `formatMediaTime`, `clampSeek` and `bufferedEnd`, with the `FormatMediaTimeOptions` and `TimeRangesLike` types, to `@ecopages/radiant-ui/helpers`.
