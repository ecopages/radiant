Add the Zed theme, inspired by the Zed editor's One Light and One Dark: flat, dense surfaces, 4px controls, hairline borders, and translucent accent hover and selection fills. Import `@ecopages/radiant-themes/zed` or `@ecopages/radiant-themes/zed/compact`, or the matching `compiled/zed.css` entries. The theme names IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono, and Lilex but bundles no font files.
