Rework Windows 95 and Mac OS Classic around their native controls: two-tone bevels, gray push buttons with the default-button frame or ring, and bevelled check boxes and radios with black marks. Their dark modes now use dark fields, which fixes white-on-white select and input text. Product themes fill dark-mode primary and destructive buttons with the product's saturated color and white text, switch tracks are visible again in Cloudflare, Vercel, and macOS, and alerts keep their tone colors.
