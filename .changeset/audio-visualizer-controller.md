Add `AudioVisualizerController` and `createAudioVisualizerController` to `@ecopages/radiant-ui/controllers`, with the `bars` (DOM `--level` or canvas) and `wave` presets, the `AudioVisualizerPreset`, `AudioFrame`, `BarsOptions` and `WaveOptions` types, and the preset utilities `logBands`, `bandPeak` and `createCanvasSurface` for writing your own. The controller runs one frame loop while an audio or video element plays and reads the sound through an analyser of its own. The first time an element is visualized its sound is routed through one shared `AudioContext` straight to the speakers, and that cannot be undone, so a visualized element must keep same-origin or CORS-enabled sources (redirects included) for its whole life: a later cross-origin source without `crossorigin` is silent and reported as the `opaque` state, and a context the browser suspends or interrupts silences the media until it runs again. The controller stays idle when the browser has not allowed the audio context to start, reports `opaque` while the media plays a cross-origin source without `crossorigin` (and builds no graph), stays at rest under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and swaps presets at runtime with `setPreset`. `refresh()` re-evaluates the getters and `getEnabled`, and `resume()` starts the context from a click or key handler (not verified on iOS or WebKit). The canvas `bars` preset takes `padding`, `segments`, `segmentGap`, `ends`, `colorEnd`, `peaks: { hold, fall }` and `idle: 'floor'` (LED-style bars with peak caps that keep drawing at rest, so they show under reduced motion), `wave` takes `padding`, `createCanvasSurface` takes an `onResize` callback, and `stepPeak`, `litSegments` and `segmentBounds` are exported for presets you write. `onState` is called on each change of `getState()`, never per frame. `getStream` visualizes a `MediaStream` instead of an element (exactly one of `getMedia` and `getStream`): its source goes to the analyser only, never to the speakers, its tracks are never stopped, it is idle while the stream has no live audio track, and `refresh()` picks up a new or `null` stream.
